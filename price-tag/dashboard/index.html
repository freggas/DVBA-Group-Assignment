Appendix · How we know

Our findings rest on all 104 matches and pre-tournament values for all 48 teams

Data

Results of all 104 matches and the stage each team reached (Kaggle, mann14). Transfermarkt squad values, FIFA ranking and bookmaker odds from April 2026 (Kaggle, khayalmammadov). Joined on team name; one record corrected (South Africa played a round-of-32 match).

Method

Spearman rank correlation of each predictor with stage reached. The price tag places squads in the round they would reach if the richer side always won. Knockout ties split by whether they were settled in play or on penalties.

Limits

One tournament, so some groups are small (7 squads above €900m, 4 shoot-outs). Market value also reflects form and club level, so the link is a correlation, not a cause. The price tag ignores the draw and the bracket.
