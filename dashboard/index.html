Squad value buys a place in the knockouts, not the trophy

The more valuable squad won 9 in 10 decided matches. But once only rich squads were left, value stopped separating them: the more valuable side lost both semi-finals. For a federation valued at €200–500m, the step that changes outcomes is reaching about €500m.

Definitions & method

Measures

  • Squad value: Transfermarkt market value of the squad, April 2026 snapshot (€m, rounded).
  • Stage reached: derived from all 104 match results. Third place counts as semi-final.
  • Expected stage: rank squads by value; #1 is expected to win, #2 to lose the final, #3–4 to reach the semi-finals, #5–8 the quarter-finals, #9–16 the Round of 16, #17–32 the Round of 32.
  • Decided match: a winner after 90 or 120 minutes. Shoot-outs count as draws.

How strong is the link?

Limits

  • One tournament, 48 teams. Association, not cause: value is a proxy for player quality.
  • FIFA ranking and bookmaker odds explain about as much as value does.
  • The expectation ignores how hard each group and bracket path was.
