Shoot-outs count by who went through; the third-place match is excluded. The median gap does not fall every round (quarter-finals 2.0× vs Round of 16 1.5×), and there were only 2 semi-finals and 1 final: read the late rounds as illustration, not proof.

Small groups (CAF 10 squads, hosts 3): a signal, not a finding. The three hosts belong to CONCACAF and account for its whole surplus. Home advantage is confounded with hosting, and 8 of the 32 Round-of-32 places went to best third-placed teams, so the draw matters too.

    The ask

    One tournament, 48 squads: the curve shows association, not cause, and its interval is wide. Market value is a proxy for player quality, not a lever you can pull directly; that a player pathway into top leagues raises it is our assumption, not something this data tests.
